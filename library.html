Library Rules & Regulations

  1. Every member must carry a valid library ID card.
  2. Books can be borrowed for a maximum of 14 days.
  3. Members must return books on or before the due date.
  4. Keep silence inside the library.
  5. Food and drinks are not allowed in the reading area.
  6. Handle all books carefully and do not damage them.
  7. Late returns may be subject to a fine.
  8. Maintain cleanliness and discipline in the library.
